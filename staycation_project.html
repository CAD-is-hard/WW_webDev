Arches National Park!

About Arches

Arches is a national park in Utah with over 2000 natural arch formatations. The park has beautiful geological formations and natural earth colors. It is also the homeland of the Hopi Tribe, Las Vegas Tribe of Paiute Indians of the Las Vegas Indian Colony, Moapa Band of Paiute Indians of the Moapa River Reservation, Navajo Nation, Rosebud Sioux Tribe of the Rosebud Sioux Reservation, Southern Ute Indian Tribe of the Southern Ute Reservation, Ute Indian Tribe of Uintah and Ouray Reservation, Ute Mountain Ute Tribe, White Mesa Ute, and Zuni Tribe of the Zuni Reservation. Originaly, Arches was a dried out seabed, but over long periods of time, natural forces weathered the rock away leading to the beautiful arch formations we have today.

Why Should You Visit Arches?

  1. Arches has the highest concentration of natural arches anywhere on earth!
  2. The Landscape Arch is 306 feet arcoss, and is the longest in North America.
  3. The famous Balanced Rock is located in the park, a 3577 pound rock balanced precariously on a thin tower.
  4. Dark Sky Park is one of the premier locations in the state for stargazing due to its preotection of natural species and rules against light pollution.
