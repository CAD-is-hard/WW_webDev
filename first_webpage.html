Why 3D Printing is great!

Introduction

Imagine if you could make any object you wanted. With 3d printing, you can almost do that. The most common type of 3d printing is FDM, or fused deposition modeling. This
type is where plastic is heated up and extruded through a nozzle to form a layer. Imagine printing out a picture of a singlis slice of an object. That is what one layer is, and it does
this hundreds of times for one model. After sometime, you have a finished part ready to use!

Uses for 3d printing

3d printing can be used for rapid prototyping of products. You can print something, test it, adjust the model, and print it again very quickly. There are also specialized
materials that you can use, such as flexible TPU, super strong PPA-CF, and even wood infused filament. You can also use it for things around the house. Print drawer
organizers, coat hangers, and many other things. You can also just make toys for fun. You can make decorations, jigs for wood working, and cosplay items. 3d printing
is a great resource that seems like it is from the future. It is being used in many fields, due to its usefulness.
